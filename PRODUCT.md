# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are companies and teams that build software and want help testing it. They land on the site to judge whether Milo van der Kruis can help them, and to get in touch. Colleagues and other testers may also visit, but the site is not written for them first.

## Product Purpose

4-testing is the company of Milo van der Kruis (Luxwoude, Netherlands). The website presents who he is and what 4-testing does: helping teams find problems in their software early, before their users do. Its job is to present the company clearly and credibly, not to run an active sales campaign. Success is that a visiting company understands what 4-testing offers and can easily reach Milo.

## Positioning

Open decision. What sets 4-testing apart from other testing providers has not been stated yet, so it must not be invented. Current copy only claims that good testing is about understanding what matters to a product's users, not just hunting for bugs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services offered (confirmed): test advice and carrying out tests. Test automation and training/coaching were explicitly not selected, so the site must not claim them.
- Pages today: Home, About, Articles, Contact. History was removed (it only held placeholders) and returns once Milo supplies real milestones. The Articles page lists curated links to third-party articles, each with a short teaser and an optional photo.
- Contact currently works through a `mailto:` form. A form service (e.g. Formspree) is planned once Milo has an endpoint.
- Hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages from a public repository.
- The site is bilingual: Dutch (default) and English, chosen with a dropdown in the menu of every page. Tone in both languages is fairly informal and simple: "je/jij" or "you" and "we", short sentences, no jargon (see `CLAUDE.md`).
- Undecided: the founding date and real History milestones, concrete service descriptions, and the price or engagement model. None of these may be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: 4-testing. Owner and only named person: Milo van der Kruis. Contact address: milo@4-testing.nl. LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/milo-van-der-kruis-2b18a73/
- Voice: informal, plain, friendly and direct, but still credible for a testing company.
- The current logo (a check mark in a rounded square) is a placeholder. Milo intends to supply his own logo.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: the LinkedIn profile link, two curated third-party articles on the Articles page, the company name and location.
- Not available and must not be fabricated: client names, testimonials or references (Milo plans to add real ones later), case studies, History milestones, prices, certifications.

## Product Principles

1. Credibility comes from being honest. Show only what is real; leave a section out or mark it as coming soon rather than invent proof.
2. Say it plainly. Short, everyday wording that a busy manager can absorb in seconds.
3. Make it easy to reach Milo. A visiting company should always be one step from contact.
4. Keep it personal and small. The site presents one person's company, not a corporate machine.
5. Stay accurate about services. Claim advice and test execution only until Milo confirms more.
